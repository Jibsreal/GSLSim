Russian Federation

General Speakers’ List Speech — Disarmament and Security in the South China Sea

Honorable Chair, distinguished delegates,

The Russian Federation views the South China Sea as a vital artery of international trade and a region where stability serves the interests of all nations. As a non-claimant State, Russia does not seek to adjudicate sovereignty disputes, but firmly believes that lasting peace can only be achieved through peaceful negotiations among the countries directly concerned, full implementation of the 2002 Declaration on the Conduct of Parties, and the early conclusion of a mutually acceptable Code of Conduct. We support ASEAN’s central role in regional security architecture and oppose external interference that risks transforming maritime disagreements into broader geopolitical confrontation. Russia calls upon this committee to prioritize confidence-building measures, maritime communication mechanisms, and practical cooperation on environmental protection and fisheries management. The South China Sea must remain a region of peaceful navigation and shared development—not a theatre of military confrontation.

Thank you.
